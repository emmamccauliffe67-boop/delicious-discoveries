♡ Recipe of the Day ♡

Classic Tiramisu

Tiramisu is a delicious Italian dessert made with coffee-soaked ladyfingers and a creamy mascarpone filling. It's the perfect treat for dessert lovers!

Preparation time: 25 minutes

Chilling time: At least 4 hours

Servings: 6

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cold heavy whipping cream
  • 8 oz mascarpone cheese, softened
  • 1/3 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 cup cooled strong coffee
  • About 18 ladyfinger cookies
  • Unsweetened cocoa powder for dusting

Instructions

  1. Brew the coffee and let it cool completely.
  2. Whip the heavy cream in a bowl until stiff peaks form.
  3. In another bowl, gently mix the mascarpone cheese, sugar, and vanilla until smooth.
  4. Fold the whipped cream into the mascarpone mixture.
  5. Quickly dip each ladyfinger into the cooled coffee.
  6. Arrange a layer of ladyfingers in a small dish.
  7. Spread half of the cream mixture over the cookies.
  8. Add another layer of coffee-dipped ladyfingers and the remaining cream.
  9. Cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours.
  10. Dust with cocoa powder before serving. Keep refrigerated.

♡ Tip: Don't soak the ladyfingers for too long, or your tiramisu may become soggy!
